Our Project

Smart Library is a student project designed to make book discovery and basic library operations easier.

Our Objectives

  • Display a catalogue of books.
  • Search books by title or author.
  • Show book availability.
  • Demonstrate borrowing and returning books.

Technology Used

HTML, CSS and JavaScript.

Future Enhancements

Secure login, librarian accounts, database integration, due dates and borrowing history.
